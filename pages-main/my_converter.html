Format Guide

Which Format Should You Choose?

PNG Format

Best for logos, illustrations, screenshots, and icons requiring sharp edges and alpha transparency channels.

JPG / JPEG

Standard for photographs and complex real-world scenes with rich gradients. Exceptional compatibility on every device.

WEBP Format

Google's ultra-efficient web format offering up to 30% smaller size than JPG with identical visual quality and transparency.
