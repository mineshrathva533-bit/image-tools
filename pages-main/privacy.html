Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our utmost priority. This document outlines how our application operates and how we protect your information whenever you utilize our tools.

Zero Permanent Storage

We never store your uploaded images on remote servers or cloud storage. All image decoding and manipulation happens securely inside your device's browser memory.

Client-Side Processing

Your image files are rendered directly on your local device CPU/GPU using modern web APIs (Canvas, Web Workers, Blob APIs), guaranteeing total confidentiality.

Cookies & Anonymous Analytics

We may use standard functional cookies to remember your theme preferences (dark/light mode) and aggregate non-identifiable web traffic statistics to improve our service.

Zero Data Monetization
