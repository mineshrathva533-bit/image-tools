The Technology

How We Deliver Studio Quality Without Server Infrastructure

Traditional online image editors require you to upload your files to remote cloud storage where backend scripts process them and send them back. This causes slow upload times, privacy risks, and bandwidth caps.

ImageTools flips this model entirely. By utilizing modern HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, and client-side optimization libraries, every compression pass, format conversion, and crop rectangle runs inside your own device’s CPU and GPU.
